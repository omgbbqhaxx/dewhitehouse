How it works

  1. 1. Propose. During the proposing period any VRNouns holder submits one proposal. The full text is stored on Base.
  2. 2. Vote. During the voting period holders split their votes across proposals. One VRNouns equals one vote.
  3. 3. Conclude. When voting ends, anyone calls the contract. It ranks proposals, pays the top ones from the treasury and marks them as winners.

No backend. No moderation queue. What you sign is what is published.
